Transformation

Replacing Paper Ledgers with Clinical Intelligence

Traditional blood banks lose units to expired stock and risk human error in eligibility checks. Here is how BloodBank solves it.

Paper Ledgers & Manual Tracking
  • ❌
    Zero Stock Visibility: Manual tallying causes stock-outs during emergency transfusions.
  • ❌
    Donor Eligibility Errors: Donors allowed to donate before 90-day recovery, endangering health.
  • ❌
    Expiry Wastage: Newer units issued first, leaving older units to expire unnoticed on shelves.
  • ❌
    Double-Issue Vulnerability: A recorded unit can be accidentally allocated to two patients.
BloodBank Automated Platform
  • ✓
    Real-Time Stock Map: Instant inventory counts across all 8 blood groups available 24/7.
  • ✓
    Server-Enforced Cooldown: 90-day interval validated in Java Service layer before persistence.
  • ✓
    Automated FEFO Issuing: First-Expire, First-Out selects the earliest safe expiring unit automatically.
  • ✓
    Unique Database Locking: Unique database constraint on issue records prevents double-issue.

Safety Invariants

Rules the System Never Bypasses

Enforced in the Java Service layer and relational database schema without compromise.

🔒
90-Day Donor Cooldown
Enforces mandatory whole-blood recovery gap. Second donations within 90 days are strictly rejected with HTTP 400.
🛡️
7-Day Near-Expiry Isolation
Units expiring within 7 days are automatically quarantined from the usable stock pool.
⛔
Zero Expired Dispensing
Units with expiry date < today are transitioned to EXPIRED and can never be issued.
⚡
Double-Issue Prevention
Unique database constraint on issue_records.blood_unit_id prevents a unit from ever being issued twice.
