Donor Eligibility Tracker

A production-grade Spring Boot system engineered for strict 90-day donor eligibility enforcement, atomic donation registration, 42-day lifecycle tracking, 7-day near-expiry isolation, and First-Expire, First-Out (FEFO) clinical blood issuing.

Open Live Dashboard Register Donor Swagger OpenAPI

90-Day Eligibility Guard

Prevents premature donation attempts by validating the mandatory 90-day recovery interval before accepting blood units.

FEFO Safe Issuing

First-Expire, First-Out algorithm automatically selects safe units with earliest expiry, eliminating inventory wastage.

7-Day Near-Expiry Filter

Units within 7 days of expiry are automatically isolated from usable stock and prohibited from being issued to patients.

Atomic Transactions

Donation intake and unit generation are wrapped in Spring JPA transactions, preventing orphan or corrupted inventory records.
